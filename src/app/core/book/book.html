Booking FAQ

Cost and Payment

How are donations spent?

Donations cover performance costs including travel expenses, equipment maintenace, and team meals.

All contributions go directly towards supporting the team and no funds received from lion dancing are used for personal benefit.

How are performance fees and donations determined?

Performance fees are often determined by the following:

  • Event Timing
  • Event Location
  • Event Type
  • Event Audience
  • Time of Event (in regards to performer availability)

Our Client Contact team will provide you with a base quote for the performance fees. This fee is the minimum required amount to cover performance costs and maintenance. Additional donations above that amount are optional.
